Character Creation Checklist

Youth, 4 skill points, max rank Basic:
• 1 must go to Body skills, 1 to Mind skills, 1 to Spirit skills, and the last may be spent anywhere.
Stirring, 3 skill points, max rank Basic:
• 1 must go to Body skills, 1 to Mind skills, 1 to Spirit skills,
Way Training, 3 skill points, max rank Trained:
• Ways each have a prequisite skill that must have a basic rank • The Way's attack skill becomes Rank 3:Trained
• You gain the Way's Talent and it determines your Primary Attribute • Skills points can be spent anywhere.
Assign Attribute Points
• The value of a primary attribute is the max value if its sub-attributes • Primary Attribute has a value of 2 and 3 points to distribute to its sub-attributes
• Select Secondary Attribute, it has a value of 2 and 2 points to distribute
• Select Tertiary Attribute, it has a value of 1 and 1 point to distribute
Abilities, 1 Talent, 2 Tricks:
• Basic rank in a skill allows you to optionally select the Talent or Tricks associated the skill.
Proficiencies, 1 per Attack Skill Rank starting at Basic.
