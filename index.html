Character Creation Checklist

Youth, 4 skill points, max rank Basic:
• 1 must go to Body skills, 1 to Mind skills, 1 to Spirit skills, and the last may be spent anywhere.
Stirring, 5 skill points, max rank Trained:
• 1 must go to a different category than Youth's free point.
• 1 must go to a different category than the first.
• 1 can be spent anywhere.
• 3 must upgrade a Basic skill to Trained.
Way:
• Set attack skill to Trained (Rank 2).
• Learn Way's Talent and Foci.
Attributes, assign priorities:
• Primary: 3 points
• Secondary: 2 points
• Tertiary: 1 point
• Distribute sub-attributes (max = Attribute value).
Skills, 3 per level + 9 starting:
• Max Rank: 3 (Expert at lvl 1, up to Grandmaster at higher).
• Passive = 2 + Rank + Mod.
Proficiencies (for Strike/Blast/Invoke):
• Slots = Skill Rank.
Gear:
• 5 Ready slots.
• Packs stow gear, containers hold liquids.
• Load limit = Brawn + 10.
Abilities (Talents/Tricks/Rituals):
• 1 per level +1 (total level +1).
• Way provides 1 Talent.
